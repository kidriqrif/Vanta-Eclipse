Vanta Eclipse

An idle RPG in the dark. Your hero fights on while you're away.

What it is

An idle RPG for Android. Tap to strike, or unlock auto-attack at level 15 and let your hero fight without you. Two worlds, a timed boss on every tenth level, six equipment slots, five relics, two companions, an arcade of seven minigames, daily quests, and the Eclipse: a prestige reset that makes the next run faster than the last.

No paywall, no energy meter. Video adverts are optional and only ever offered for a bonus. Plays in portrait, one-handed, and keeps earning while you're away.

How it's built

React 18, TypeScript and Vite, wrapped for Android with Capacitor. The whole game ruleset is a pure, unit-tested reducer in src/game/; the screens only read state and dispatch actions.
