Vanta Eclipse

An idle RPG in the dark. Your hero fights on while you're away.

What it is

An idle RPG for Android, built in Godot 4.7. Tap to strike, or unlock the auto-attacker and let your hero fight without you. two worlds, a boss on every tenth gate, seven equipment slots, five relics, two pets, an arcade of four real minigames, and a prestige reset that makes the next run faster than the last.

No paywall, no energy meter, no timer blocking a door. Plays in portrait, one-handed, offline.

The art is generated

Every sprite, the bitmap font, the icons and all 16 sounds are produced by programs in tools/ rather than drawn or recorded. A palette change is one edit and one command, not 53 files reopened by hand. The palette is closed at 16 colours and the validation sweep fails on any pixel outside it — and on any asset that no longer matches the program that draws it.
