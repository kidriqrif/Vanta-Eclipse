Privacy Policy

Vanta Eclipse · last updated on publication

The short version

Vanta Eclipse does not collect your personal information. It has no account system, asks for no name or email, contains no analytics, and makes no network requests of any kind. Your progress is saved on your own device and is never sent anywhere.

What the game stores, and where

Your progress — level, currencies, equipment, relics, pets, quest state and settings — is written to a single file in the app’s private storage on your device. Nothing else reads it. It leaves your device only if you choose to back it up yourself through Android’s own backup system.

Uninstalling the app deletes that file. There is no cloud copy, so uninstalling loses your progress permanently.

Permissions

The app declares three, and uses them narrowly:

  • VIBRATE — haptic feedback on taps, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — declared for planned store and advertising functionality. The current version makes no network requests.

Children

Vanta Eclipse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, contact us at the address below.

Your rights

Because no personal data is held on any server, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request. Clearing the app’s data, or uninstalling it, removes everything the game stored.

If this app changes

This policy describes the app as published today. If a future version adds advertising, in-app purchases, cloud saves or analytics, those services will collect data of their own, and this policy will be updated before that version ships. The updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
