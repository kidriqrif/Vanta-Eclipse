Privacy Policy

Vanta Eclipse · last updated 2026-08-10

The short version

Vanta Eclipse keeps your game progress on your own device. It has no account system, asks for no name or email, and sends none of your progress anywhere.

The game shows optional rewarded video adverts, supplied by Google AdMob. AdMob is a separate company with its own policies, and it does collect data in order to select and measure those adverts. That is described in full below, and you can opt out of personalised advertising at any time from your device settings.

What the game itself stores

Your progress — level, currencies, equipment, relics, pets, quest state, settings and purchase records — is written to a single file in the app's private storage on your device. Nothing else reads it. It leaves your device only if you choose to back it up yourself through Android's own backup system.

Uninstalling the app deletes that file. There is no cloud copy, so uninstalling loses your progress permanently.

What Google AdMob collects

Adverts in this game are always optional. They are offered in exchange for a bonus, never forced, never shown between screens, and declining one costs you nothing you had already earned. You can play the entire game without watching a single advert.

When you are shown one, Google may collect and process:

DataWhy
Advertising ID To select adverts, cap how often you see the same one, and measure whether an advert was watched. This is a resettable identifier, not a permanent one.
Approximate location, derived from your IP address To serve adverts appropriate to your country and comply with regional advertising law. Not precise location; the app requests no location permission.
Device and app information Device model, operating system version, language, and the identity of this app. Used for advert compatibility and fraud prevention.
Advert interaction events Whether an advert was requested, shown, watched to completion, or dismissed.

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's own policies:

  • How Google uses data in advertising
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob and children's privacy

We do not receive your Advertising ID, your IP address, or any profile built from them. What reaches us is an aggregate count of adverts shown, and a yes/no answer per advert so the game knows whether to grant the reward.

Your choices

  • Watch nothing. Every advert in this game is opt-in. No mechanic, level, item or currency is locked behind one.
  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads. You can also switch on Opt out of Ads Personalisation there, which stops adverts being selected from your interests.
  • Clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove everything the game stored on your device.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details — Google handles the transaction and tells the app only whether it succeeded. The app records which items you own so it can restore them; that record lives in the same local save file as the rest of your progress.

Permissions

The app declares these, and uses them narrowly:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to fetch adverts and to complete purchases. The game itself makes no other network request.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — the Advertising ID described above, required by the advert SDK on Android 13 and later.
  • VIBRATE — haptic feedback on taps, which you can turn off in Settings.

Children

Vanta Eclipse is not directed at children under 13, is not listed in Google Play's Designed for Families programme, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The advert SDK is configured to treat this app as a general-audience app. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Your rights

We hold no personal data on any server, so there is nothing for us to export or delete on request. For the data Google collects through AdMob, use the Google controls linked above, or contact Google directly — they are the data controller for it.

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or a US state with its own privacy law, the advert SDK will ask for your consent, or offer you a way to refuse, before any personalised advert is shown. Refusing means you see non-personalised adverts rather than none.

If this app changes

This policy describes the app as published today. If a future version adds cloud saves, analytics, or any other data collection, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
